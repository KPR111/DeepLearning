This Architecture is a Mixture of CVAE and Transformers

In traditional VAE , we have an encoder and decoder
But this VAE is completely customzied
i.e 
Firstly it's normal VAE it's conditioned VAE 
Secondly the encoder don't use the normal MLP , instead it uses the Transformers self attention along with CLS token -> [Replica of ViT architecture]
so this encoder returns a cls token , aka Z which contains all compressed information of k chunks of future actions and the current state

Wait a minute what do you mean by K chunks of future actions?

ACTION CHUNKING IDEA :-
This idea is commonly used in all the models released 
Problem : previously we were only predicting one action at a time , which leading to the jerking of the robot and it's not smooth , apart from this there is issue of
1. Compounding Errors (The Drift Problem)
2. Failure to Capture Non-Markovian Human Behavior (The "Pause" Problem)
3. Causal Misattribution (Over-indexing on Proprioception)
The ACT Fix: Predicting an entire trajectory forces the network to actually use the visual context (the camera frames) to plan out where the robot needs to go over
the next 1–2 seconds.

Comming back to the Architecture
The VAE Decoder is completely changed
It agains contains an encoder and decoder , both are Transformers based

So in Encoder of the Decoder 
We will have Z , Current State , Images (visual information) into the Self Attention -> we get a context vector of entire context
So in Decoder of the Decoder
we will be having the entire context vector , and also we send the object queries [DETR IDEA] 
Here object queries means K action queries , i.e we try to predict next K actions , so for that first we have to initialize them and send them to the Cross Attention
Here q of [q,k,v of attn] is from k actions and keys and values from the context vector generated from the second encoder

So as you know the attention mechanism returns the same dimesion of queries as the output 
so there we get a k context vectors of output each representing the action
