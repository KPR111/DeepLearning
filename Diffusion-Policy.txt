Diffusion Policy

Let's Start with the limitations of the ACT 

1.MultiModality Collapse
ACT unable to take mixture of gussians , and tries to represent all actions in a single Gussian Distribution , which ends up taking up the average of multiple 
gussians into a single gussian
2.OOD problem
ACT struggles to perform ,when we provide it a scenario where which is not present in the distribution i.e Out of Distribution
3. High Sensitivity to Human "Data Noise"
Human demonstrators are imperfect. When teleoperating a robot, a human might hesitate, shake their hand slightly, or take a messy route. ACT tries to reconstruct the
exact data too rigidly. It ends up copying human hesitation or jitter. Diffusion Policy ignores this noise because its denoising process naturally filters
out random hand shakes.
4.VAE architecture are highly instable and easily collpased to underfitting or overfitting if not carefull

How the Diffusion Policy is solving the above limitations

1. How it Fixes Multimodality (No More Averaging)
The Old Way (ACT): ACT uses a single Gaussian curve. It must pick one average center point, which forces it to combine different demonstration styles.
The Diffusion Fix: Diffusion Policy is an iterative sampler. It uses a neural network (like a U-Net) to predict and remove 
noise. Mathematically, this allows it to shape the action landscape into multiple distinct "valleys" (called a multimodal distribution)

Human Data:    [Left Path]                      [Right Path]
                  \                                /
ACT:               \---> [Averages the Middle] <---/  ❌ (Fails)
                  
Diffusion:         /---> Snaps to Left Path           ✔️ (Succeeds)
           [Noise]
                   \---> Snaps to Right Path          ✔️ (Succeeds)


2. How it Fixes OOD and Compounding Errors (Receding Horizon Control)
The Old Way (ACT): ACT predicts a long chunk of actions and blindly executes it. If it drifts off course, the tiny error multiplies because it isn't checking the
 camera often enough.
The Diffusion Fix: Diffusion Policy uses a highly reactive setup called Receding Horizon Control.

3. How it Fixes Human "Data Noise" (Natural Smoothing)
The Diffusion Fix: Because the entire core of Diffusion Policy is about smoothing out noise, it treats human hesitation or tiny hand jitters as just "random noise".
 During the denoising steps, the model naturally filters out these shaky movements, resulting in a robot trajectory that is often much smoother and cleaner than the
 original human demonstration.
 
 
Receding horizon control: A control strategy where the robot plans a sequence of future actions, executes only the first few actions, and then replans. 
This fixes errors in real-time.

DDPM is must to understand this Diffusion Policy 

Basic Summary of DDPM 
In forward pass we take a fresh image and keep on adding noise with noise schedule alpha , beta until we active complete pure noise i.e xt+1 = alpha*xt + beta*epsilon
where here aplha and beta are the noise schedule
While Denoisng the Image we have xt-1 = xt - constant*E(thetha,time) + beta*epsilon
this is not the orginal formula , I have removed all constants from it , like alpha ,alphabars and betas , betabars


While DDPMs are typically used for image generation (x is an image), we use a DDPM to learn robot visuomotor policies. 
This requires two major modifications in the formulation: 
1. changing the output x to represent robot actions. 
2. making the denoising processes conditioned on input observation Ot(The state of the robot - DOF at timestamp t or Images of robot at timestamp t)


So in this Diffusion Policy 
the U-Net part in diffusion is replaced with either  Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM) or the Trasnformers
Obviously Trasnformers work better with their usual trade offs , need more compute , more latency... blah blah ...

We will talk only about the trasnformer version of the diffusion policy

So Diffusion Policy is a replica of DDPM 
with the following changes

1.Image noising and densoing is replaced with Tp Actions noising and denoising
		-To the latest To states of the robo (To stands a number i.e T0 =5 , i.e last 5 states of the robot)
        -Tp while generating the Actions , we generate Tp number actions (to remove jerks and smooth motion of robot arm)
		-Ta In total Tp generated Actions , we execute only Ta number of actions on robot arm and discard remaining (this reduces errors like compounding error)
2.So Inforward pass ,we take a Action and conditioned with the observation Ot and try to add noise until we get a pure guassian noise
		-Ot at time step t the policy takes the latest/last To steps of observation data Ot (Ot include both state info(dof info or propriceptory data) and Image info)
the formula changes to At-1 = At - comstant*E(thetha,Ot,time) + gussian noise i.e conditioned with Ot

Let's study what does it mean by conditioned with Ot

Forward Pass
		Pick A0 and add noise i.e A1 = alpha*A0 + beta*epsilon
		there is no intervention of Ot here
		
Denoisng*** - 
	We take a pure gussian noise and start conditioned denoising it until we get consistent reliable action as output
		
	Input is Obviously the At_t+Tp
		-Here At_t+Tp means denoising all Tp number of consective actions , not one action at a time [Even noising is also done for Tp actions at once]
	
	Condition Ot => includes last To steps of observation data [Include Image data , State_dim] of last To steps 
		Image data - [B,To,C,H,W] => [B,To,feat_dim]
		State/Dof/Propriceptory data - [B,To,State_dim]   (Note - state_dim and action_dim are same)
		Ot => concat(Image data , state data) => [B,To,feat_dim+state_dim] =linear projetion=> [B,To,d_model]    (Note - concat doesn't mean adding)
		
	U-Net replaced with Trasnformers
		where we will be having blocks 
		Each block having a self attention and followed by a cross attention
		Queries => [B,Tp,Action_dim] =linear projection=> [B,Tp,d_model]
		In self attention => Values and Keys are same i.e [B,Tp,d_model]
		In cros attetnion => Values and Keys are from i.e [B,To,d_model]
		final context vector is of dimension of => [B,Tp,d_model]
	(note :- In cross attention the number of token may be different , but the embedding dimension should be same inorder to perform attention ,no of tokens tp,to)
